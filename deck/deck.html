01 · The problem

India grows the most storied pepper in the world and exports almost none of it

The 2024/25 crop fell to a record-low ~48,000 t while domestic consumption ran ~81,000 t. India net-imported pepper.

What does leave the country leaves as anonymous bulk. A buyer in Rotterdam cannot prove the bag they bought came from Kerala at all — and the market's answer to that is cheaper pepper from somewhere else, re-bagged.

04 · How it works

The lot gate

  1. Signed supplier declaration — no ethylene-oxide fumigation
  2. Moisture and water activity measured at intake
  3. Full panel: mycotoxins, microbiology, heavy metals, pesticide residues, EtO
  4. Grade verified by sieve and density

Only then does a lot receive a number and enter sellable stock. This is the whole company. Everything else is logistics.

Ethylene oxide is banned for food use in the EU and measured with 2-chloroethanol. There is no market where a positive result is a negotiable threshold. We test in India before it ships.

10 · Moat

Why this is defensible and not just trading

  • Lot-level chain of custody with named grower clusters — expensive to build, boring to copy
  • Rejection data fed back to clusters, so our supply improves and the spot market's does not
  • A brand that prints its own test results — a liability for anyone competing on price alone
  • Two channels on one lot gate — D2C carries margin, B2B carries predictable volume

A trader can undercut our price. A trader cannot undercut our paperwork without doing the work.

13 · Risk

What actually kills this company

Ethylene oxide contamination in incoming lots

Contract clause with every supplier: no EtO fumigation. Test every lot before it leaves India. Steam treatment only. Reject-on-positive with cost recovery.

Aflatoxin and ochratoxin A above EU limit

Moisture spec max 12% at intake, covered drying, per-lot mycotoxin panel, reject lots from suppliers with two failures in a season.

Salmonella detection

Validated steam treatment, post-treatment re-test, sealed liners, no re-bagging in open air.

Retail price positioning sits above the visible reference points

Never sell against a kilo bag on price. Merchandise the 100 g trial pack as the entry point, put the lot number and panel on the front of the pack, and let the B2B channel carry volume. Track conversion by pack size; if the 500 g underperforms the 100 g for two quarters, reprice the 500 g toward EUR 58/kg rather than discount the brand.

14 · Why now

Zero duty, a depleted crop, an under-built premium story

  • Whole pepper enters the EU and the US at 0% duty
  • India's FY 2024-25 spice exports hit a record $4.72 B — but pepper is only ~3% of that basket by value
  • The 2024/25 crop collapse made provenance a buying criterion, not a nice-to-have

Buyers with budget have started asking for the test panel. Most suppliers cannot produce one on demand.
