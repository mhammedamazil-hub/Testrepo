# Sky environment

- **Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy**, by **Greg Zaal / Poly Haven**.
- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy
- License: **CC0-1.0**, https://polyhaven.com/license
- Official source: 4k Radiance HDRI. Exact original SHA-256, dimensions and API provenance are retained in `sky_provenance.json`.
- The repository contains a compact, ACES-style tone-mapped JPEG derivative for the visible distant sky and matching water reflections. It is packed into the Blender scene.
- Physically directed sunrise, atmospheric scattering, mountain/forest geometry, water waves and reflections remain rendered in Blender. This is **not** a photograph of the landscape projected onto the scene.

The original capture is daytime; the visible sky is combined with separately controlled morning solar/sky illumination. This is an art-directed environment workflow, not a claim that the photographic sky was captured at the location or time of the simulated sunrise.
