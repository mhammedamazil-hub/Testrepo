Short hop: J launches from the ground with at least 85% gas. Space adds lift, Ctrl/C reduces it, Shift adds thrust, L requests descent. Every thruster uses the same finite tank; nothing refills in air. Automatic descent after 2.8s; hard powered limit 6s. Ground refill takes 20s. These are game settings, not Tesla specifications. Drive mode: 3-second launch countdown, three sector splits and clean-lap PB medals. Hops/recovery/changed assist make the current lap practice-only. Normal driving remains force-driven. Both steering modes support simultaneous pedals and camera touch. Road assist limits requested torque and brakes for bends; it never assigns vehicle position or speed. Responsive full-screen view; 60 FPS is a target, not a certified device benchmark. Art-directed moonlight keeps the road readable. No Steam account or affiliation.
